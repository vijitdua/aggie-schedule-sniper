Aggie Schedule Sniper

Automatically hit "Register ALL" Clicks Register ALL when your pass time hits. Also works for 4 hours after your pass-time for any unregistered courses.
Show pass-time countdown Shows a countdown on Schedule Builder until your pass time.
Keep me logged in Attempts to keep you logged in even when idle. UC Davis may still sign you out after a few hours.
RateMyProfessors reviews Shows rating, would-take-again, and difficulty below instructor names in search results and your saved schedule.

Warning — proceed with caution

This menu is for developers who know what they’re doing. Changing these settings can easily break registration, calendar export, session keepalive, or other features.

Aggie Schedule Sniper is open source — view the source on GitHub if you want to understand an option before changing it.

If something stops working after you edit overrides, reset everything back to the defaults.
